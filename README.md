## About This Commentary

As conversational voice AI becomes more accessible, an application's ability to conduct a natural conversation may become less distinctive. Using healthcare appointment scheduling as a practical example, this commentary examines why understanding a caller's request is different from completing a reliable business workflow, and how communications infrastructure, AI interpretation, application controls, and systems of record are converging.

The article offers an interpretation of observed technology and market developments rather than a forecast of competitive winners or losers.
